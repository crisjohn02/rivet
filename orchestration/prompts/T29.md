The rules, from spec §16.4 and the contract:
- A FULL emitted segment suppresses every candidate whose span lies wholly inside it. A signature is a derived summary, not a source slice, so it suppresses nothing by containment.
- A related ancestor of an emitted segment must not repeat that segment's body. It may be emitted as a signature that does not overlap, otherwise it is skipped. Under `--collapse never` it has no signature form and is skipped.
- A container's signature summary omits member declarations already emitted as separate segments.
- At most `--limit` segments are emitted, default 50, target included.
- Estimates are computed on the FINAL emitted representations, after overlap removal.
- Omission counts cover only explored unique candidates, each assigned exactly once, with precedence overlap, then limit, then budget.

The recommended approach: when a member is emitted separately, rebuild the already-emitted container summary without that member and re-estimate it. That only ever lowers the total, so it cannot break the budget, and the freed tokens are available to later candidates.
